Connect your Android phone

  1. On the phone: Settings → About phone → tap Build number seven times.
  2. Settings → System → Developer options → turn on USB debugging.
  3. Plug it in with a data cable and accept the Allow USB debugging prompt.
  4. No cable? Use Wireless debugging (Android 11+) and pair from the right.
  5. Emulator (LDPlayer, BlueStacks)? Enable its ADB setting, then connect to 127.0.0.1:5555.
